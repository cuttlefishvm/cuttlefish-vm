Job bookkeeping: status, results, and the event log.

§Why events are logged and not merely broadcast

tokio::sync::broadcast has no replay for late subscribers. send returns early when the receiver count is zero and does not even write to the ring buffer, and subscribe starts a receiver at the current tail.

That is fatal for the obvious client shape: submit a job, then connect to its event stream. Those are two round trips, and a fast job finishes during the gap — so a broadcast-only design delivers nothing, reliably, and looks like a streaming bug rather than a design one.

So every event goes into a per-job log as well as the live channel, and subscribing hands back the backlog together with a receiver. Finished envelopes are retained for the same reason: a dropped connection must not be able to lose a result that the daemon already computed.
