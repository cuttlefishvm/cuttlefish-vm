Expand description

Serve app on a unix socket at sock_path until shutdown resolves.

axum 0.8’s serve accepts any listener, so this is a handful of lines. On 0.7 it took a concrete TcpListener and a unix socket needed a hand-rolled hyper accept loop over hyper-util and tower; if you find yourself reintroducing that, check the axum version first.
